Kartiki Strategic Capital - website (HTML, CSS, Bootstrap 5.3, vanilla JS)

HOW TO RUN: open index.html in a browser (internet needed for Bootstrap CDN and Google Fonts).

PAGES: index.html, services.html, calculators.html, about.html, contact.html
FILES: css/style.css (all custom styling), js/main.js (shared header/footer, service data, calculators, animations)

BEFORE LAUNCH:
1. Replace placeholder phone, email, address and hours in contact.html.
2. Check the stats on index.html (15+ years, 2,500+ families) - they are placeholders.
3. Contact form shows a thank-you message only. Connect it to PHP/MySQL or email to receive enquiries.
4. Header and footer are injected by js/main.js. For production you can move them into PHP includes.
5. Services text lives in the S array in js/main.js.
